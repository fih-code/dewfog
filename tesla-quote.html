tesla quote

borrowed name · missing source · real signal
Tesla really did spend decades on frequency and resonance — a real 1900 patent, a real Colorado Springs lab reporting tens of millions of volts. The famous line about "the secrets of the universe," though, traces to a single uncorroborated account from a man who was, at the same time, marketing a "Tesla Purple Energy Plate." No biography, patent, or lecture contains it. Strip the fake quote away and a genuine question is still standing behind it: does measurable frequency matter to consciousness? A small number of credentialed scientists think so, in ways much more modest than the quote implies — real oscillations in the brain, published in real journals, still an unresolved minority position, not a settled answer.
